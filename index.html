01 · Profile

Toward agents that improve through experience.

My current research centers on agentic coding and agent self-evolution: how coding agents can accumulate reusable experience, refine their own skills, and improve through interaction with tasks and environments. I am also interested in the data and evaluation systems required to measure this progress reliably.
