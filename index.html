About Me

I am a Master's student at Zhejiang University, working on agentic coding and agent self-evolution. I am interested in building agents that learn from experience, adapt to new tasks, and improve over time.

Currently, I am a research intern with the AIData Team at Alibaba Group, mentored by Xander Xu. Previously, I was a research intern at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out!

Research Interests

  • Agentic Coding: coding agents that solve tasks through interaction with tools and environments.
  • Agent Self-Evolution: accumulating reusable experience and refining agent skills.
  • Reliable Evaluation: data and evaluation systems that measure agent progress.

Selected Project

EvoPatient

LLM Agents · Healthcare · Evaluation

A multi-agent standardized-patient evolution environment for long-horizon clinical interviews. Patient and dynamically recruited Doctor Agents reuse experience through attention and trajectory-library memory.

Evaluated on 150 cases and about 3,000 doctor questions, with a 12.7% improvement in Ability over few-shot prompting.
